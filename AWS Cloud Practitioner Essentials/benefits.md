1. **Pay as You Go**: Instead of paying a large amount upfront for hardware and data centers, you only pay for the resources you use. 
   - *Example*: Think of it like renting a car. You only pay for the days you use it, rather than buying a car that you might not drive every day.

2. **Economies of Scale**: AWS buys hardware in bulk, which allows them to get lower prices and pass those savings on to you.
   - *Example*: Imagine buying groceries in bulk at a wholesale store. You save money because you’re buying a larger quantity, just like AWS saves money by purchasing lots of servers.

3. **Stop Guessing Capacity**: With AWS, you don’t have to predict how much hardware you’ll need. You can easily scale up or down based on actual demand.
   - *Example*: It’s like having a flexible gym membership. If you know you’ll be going more often, you can upgrade your plan, but if you’re busy, you can downgrade without losing money.

4. **Increase Speed and Agility**: You can quickly set up test environments and try new ideas without a long wait.
   - *Example*: Think of it like cooking. If you want to try a new recipe, you can quickly gather ingredients and experiment without needing to buy a whole new kitchen.

5. **Stop Spending on Maintenance**: AWS takes care of the servers, so you can focus on your business instead of worrying about hardware upkeep.
   - *Example*: It’s like hiring a cleaning service for your home. You pay them to keep everything tidy, allowing you to spend your time on things you enjoy.

6. **Go Global in Minutes**: You can expand your business to different countries without needing to set up new data centers.
   - *Example*: Imagine you want to sell your handmade crafts in another country. Instead of shipping everything yourself, you can use an online platform that handles international sales for you.
